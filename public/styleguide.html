Draw Steel Companion

The design system. Every colour here is sampled from Draw_Steel_Heroes_v1.pdf rather than invented: paper #FFFEF6, ink #0D0D0F, bronze #866D4B, chip black #000000. The book has exactly one accent colour, and so do we.

Components read only from the semantic layer. If you are writing a hex code, a px font-size, or an off-scale gap anywhere outside tokens.css, you are re-creating the drift this system exists to prevent — scripts/check-design-system.py will fail the build.

State hues

Interest — the one ordinal scale

Negotiation interest is a single quantity measured 1 → 5, so it is one hue getting darker, not five hues. It replaced a red → orange → gold → teal → gold rainbow that encoded magnitude as if it were identity and borrowed two live status colours for its top two steps. Every step prints its own number, so the ink flips to paper at step 3 to hold AA. Any future ordinal scale — encounter difficulty, montage challenge tier — uses this shape, not a rainbow.
